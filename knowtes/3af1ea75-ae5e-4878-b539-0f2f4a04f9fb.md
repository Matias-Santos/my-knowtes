# AI Flow Test: Multi-Note Recording

This recording was made to validate the new AI-powered note flow. The key capability being tested is the ability to record several notes in succession and send them to the server without losing flow — meaning the process should feel continuous and frictionless, not requiring the user to stop, wait, or reset between notes.

## Insight

Maintaining flow during note capture is critical to usefulness. If the pipeline can handle batched or sequential recordings seamlessly, it removes a major friction point and makes the tool viable for real-time thinking and capture during busy or creative moments.
